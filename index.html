Headmaster: A Very Sorry Muggle
(Order of Merlin, Zero Class, Chief Offender, Grand Fool of the Wizengamot)
Dear Divakshi,

Unlike the letter you may have been hoping for, this one doesn’t say you’ve been accepted to Hogwarts. It says something far more important: I am sorry. Truly, deeply sorry.

For the past few days I haven’t been there for you the way I should have been. I let meetings, work and my own busy head swallow the time that should have been ours — and you were left waiting, wondering, and feeling like you came last. You should never have felt that. Not even for a minute.

Let me say exactly what happened, because you deserve honesty and not excuses. The other day, after my meeting, I called you — and we got only five or ten minutes before I was gone again. When I finally called back, thirty minutes had passed. By then you were hurt and angry, and you didn’t want to talk to me.

And you were right. If I had been in your place, I would have felt exactly the same. You weren’t being dramatic or difficult — you were asking for the one thing I should have been giving you freely: my time and my attention.

I’ve been treating time as if I owned a Time-Turner, as if I could always go back and make it up to you later. But I can’t. It isn’t that I don’t have time for you — it’s that I’ve been spending it badly. And moments with you only come once.

So here is what I solemnly swear (and this time, I am definitely not up to no good):

  • If I can’t talk, I’ll tell you exactly when I’ll call back — and I’ll call right on time.
  • No more rushed, half-present calls. When I’m with you, I’ll truly be with you.
  • Every single day, a little time that belongs only to us — phone away, mind here.
  • And when you’re upset, I’ll listen first, before I try to explain myself.

You don’t have to forgive me today. Take all the time you need. But whenever you’re ready, I’ll be right here — not after a meeting, not thirty minutes later, not “in a bit.” Just here.

You make even my most ordinary days feel a little bit magical, Divakshi, and I never want to be the reason that magic goes quiet.

“After all this time?”
“Always.”
Yours, now and always,
Your Muggle

P.S. I promise I’ll pick up when you call. And if you’d rather reply by owl… I’ll be waiting by the window. 🦉

If you can find it in your heart to forgive me, whisper the spell — and let’s mend things.
Thank you, Divakshi. 💛
